Aims & Scope

JOMAAIR welcomes original research, case studies and review articles in the following areas:

  • Artificial intelligence applications in electrical, industrial and engineering systems
  • Project management and construction management
  • Operations, supply chain and industrial facility management
  • Data analytics and data-driven decision making
  • Emerging technologies in business and organisations
  • Interdisciplinary studies linking management and AI
